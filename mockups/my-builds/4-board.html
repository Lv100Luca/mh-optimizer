Idea 4 · A loadout board you fill from a palette

  1. 1The build is a row of seven tiles, like the game's equipment screen. A tile shows the piece, its skills, set and group, and one square per slot. Empty tiles are drop targets.
  2. 2You drag a piece from the palette onto its tile. The tile lights up and shows a ghost of what you are about to equip, so you see the swap before you let go.
  3. 3The skill strip replaces the sheet: targets first as have/want, green when met, red when short, then the other skills, free slots and set piece counts. One glance, no scrolling.
  4. 4The palette below holds everything you can equip. Tabs switch between armor, jewels, talismans and whole sets; pills narrow by kind and rarity. The kind pill follows what you drag or click. Cards are sorted by how many target levels they add; cards that add none are dimmed.
  5. 5No drag needed: a click puts the piece into its kind. If that tile is taken, you are asked; Shift-click swaps without asking.
  6. 6Not solved here: jewels need a second pass per tile (switch the palette to Jewels, then drop into the small squares). The full sheet is reduced to a strip, so the pips and "where to get it" of idea 1 are gone. Drag and drop is a lot of code in Blazor. Any damage number on this page is illustrative.
