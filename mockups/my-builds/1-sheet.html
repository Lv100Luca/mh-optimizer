Idea 1 · A live skill sheet next to the editor

  1. 1The editor stays as it is (one row per piece, pickers per slot). It moves into the left column; everything that is analysis, not drafting, folds up below it.
  2. 2The skill sheet on the right is always in view. Targets first, met in green, short in amber; the pips show the level, blue pips are jewels, the dashed ones are what is still wanted.
  3. 3Free slots and set / group piece counts sit under the skills, so you see the budget while you pick.
  4. 4Click a skill: where to get it. Jewels first (with the slot they need), then pieces by level. "Equip" fills an empty kind, "Swap" replaces what is worn, "worn" marks what the draft already has.
  5. 5The score bar keeps the draft's damage and what the last change did to it. Damage numbers are illustrative.
  6. 6Not solved here: filling the rest for you. That is idea 2; both can live together (a button in the sheet).
