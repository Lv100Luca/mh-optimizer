Idea 2 · Lock what you want, the optimizer fills the rest

  1. 1Fill the rest runs the optimizer with the kept pieces as the only Allowed armor of their kind. That is what Results already does when you lock a piece (IncludeArmor holds one piece per kind), so the solver needs no new mode.
  2. 2Each armor row and the talisman get a keep toggle. Kept rows are tinted. The weapon is always kept; it comes from the Weapon tab.
  3. 3Rows you did not fill are drawn ghosted and say what will happen to them. Same for empty jewel slots: the two Critical jewels are kept, the third weapon slot is left to the solver.
  4. 4Three knobs: which targets the run uses (this draft's sheet or the Targets tab), whether placed jewels stay, and whether the solver may re-jewel kept pieces.
  5. 5The run returns a short strip of completions, not a full results list. Kept rows read "kept", new rows show piece and jewels. Take it writes the completion into the draft; Keep as draft adds it as a new build next to it. Damage numbers are illustrative.
  6. 6Not solved here: you still need to know which pieces to lock. Nothing on this page helps you browse pieces or skills. Idea 1 or 3 covers that; this idea is the finishing move.
  7. 7Variant B: the idea scales down. With all five pieces kept the same button only fills the jewels, which is a much faster run.
