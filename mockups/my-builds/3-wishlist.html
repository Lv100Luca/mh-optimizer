Idea 3 · Start from the skills you want

  1. 1"+ New" opens a draft with no pieces. The tab shows no damage yet; nothing is scored until the draft is complete. The first thing you see is a wishlist, not an empty equipment list.
  2. 2One row per wanted skill: a stepper for the level and a coverage strip. Green squares come from picked pieces, blue from jewels the free slots could hold, dashed amber is still missing. The count on the right is have/want.
  3. 3Add skills by search or from quick chips: skills the hunter's other builds carry. A skill never has to be typed twice.
  4. 4"So far" is the running total: picked pieces, wanted levels covered, slots left, and the jewels that would close the gap with the slot level each needs. Computed from the real pieces and jewels.
  5. 5Step 2 ranks every real piece per kind by how many wanted levels it adds to what is already picked, plus a little for slots. Click one to pick it; the list below re-ranks around it. Picked pieces stay on top of their column.
  6. 6From here: open the draft in the editor to place jewels and pick a talisman, or hand the wishlist to the optimizer as targets (idea 2).
  7. 7Not solved: set bonuses and group skills are not in the wishlist, and a wishlist that no armor can satisfy needs a clear message instead of an amber strip that never fills. Levels and slots are computed from the real data; any damage number here is illustrative.
