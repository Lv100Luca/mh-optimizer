Round 2 · Board + skill sheet + fill the rest

After the first round: the sheet of idea 1 was the most readable (its "where to get it" list was too much), idea 2 is wanted, and the board and palette of idea 4 suit browsing and making things up. This page is the three together. Ideas 3, 5 and 6 were not picked.

  1. 1The board is the editor. Click a tile to select it (Arms here); the palette narrows to that kind. Each tile has a keep toggle for fill the rest; kept tiles are tinted. Empty tiles say the optimizer may fill them.
  2. 2Click an empty slot square on a tile and the palette switches to jewels of that level and kind (the Jewels state is shown small at the bottom). The board wraps to two rows beside the sheet; that is on purpose, the tiles need the width for names and slots.
  3. 3The palette: search, Armor / Jewels / Talismans, kind pills (Arms set by the selected tile, gold), skill pills (Weakness Exploit set from the sheet, teal), "helps a target" sort. A click equips into the selected tile; drag onto a tile works too.
  4. 4The skill sheet stays on the right, sticky, without the source list. Clicking a skill row toggles it as a palette filter instead: same job, no extra list.
  5. 5Fill the rest lives under the sheet, where the targets it works toward are. Completions appear as cards above the palette; "Take it" fills the unkept tiles.
  6. 6Conditions, damage per attack, upgrades and compare fold below the palette. Damage numbers are illustrative.
  7. 7Costs: a new editor component, a virtualized palette grid (hundreds of pieces), drag and drop in Blazor is optional work, the talisman keep needs a new solver option. Not solved: a build with 15+ skills makes the sheet long; a phone layout stacks everything.
