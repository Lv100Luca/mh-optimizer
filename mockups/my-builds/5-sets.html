Idea 5 · Build from sets, then fine-tune

  1. 1You browse sets, not pieces. One card per armor set from the game data: its set bonus with the piece steps, its group skill, the five pieces with their skills in short. Search and pills narrow the grid. "Rarity 8" is on; the rarity 7 sets are one click away.
  2. 2Click a piece tile to wear it. Tiles the draft wears light up. A kind another set already fills is struck through, so you see what this set would push out. All 5 and Best 2 for the bonus fill a set in one go; the best 2 are the pieces with the most of your target skills.
  3. 3Sets you wear float to the top of the grid, so what is in play never scrolls away.
  4. 4Your loadout: the seven slots as a compact list, always in view. The x takes a piece off. Legs and talisman are still empty here.
  5. 5Set bonus tracker: one row per bonus or group skill the draft touches. The weapon counts as one piece for Soul of the Dark Knight in this app, so weapon plus two Bale pieces is 3 of 4: Dark Arts is active, The Blackest Night needs one more.
  6. 6Skills: the sheet from idea 1, cut to the wanted skills, plus free slots. Same pips, same colors.
  7. 7Not solved: jewels. Nothing here places them, the editor still does that. Sets below rarity 7 are many and most are noise, so they are hidden outright. The card grid takes a lot of room, like browsing layered armor. All damage numbers are illustrative.
