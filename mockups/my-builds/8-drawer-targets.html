Round 3 · Palette as a drawer, targets from the weapon plus the draft's own

Two changes to round 2. The palette was always open and took the page; now it opens only while something is selected. And the sheet said "target" without saying whose: here the weapon's targets (Targets & limits tab) show by default and the draft adds or overrides its own, marked like per-build conditions are today.

  1. 1Nothing selected: the page is board, completions and the sheet (sketched small at the bottom). The palette is not on screen.
  2. 2Select a tile or a slot square and the palette drawer opens right under the board, pointing at the tile, with its kind preset. Esc, the ✕ or clicking the tile again closes it. Picking a card equips and keeps the drawer open for the next pick; clicking elsewhere closes it.
  3. 3The drawer's grid is capped in height and scrolls inside, so completions and the folds never jump far.
  4. 4Targets in the sheet carry a source: weapon comes from Targets & limits and is what the optimizer searches for; own is saved with this build only. Weakness Exploit shows an override: the weapon wants 3, this draft wants 5.
  5. 5"+ add a target" and the ✕ on hover edit the draft's own list. Removing an override falls back to the weapon's level. The weapon's targets are edited where they live, with a link.
  6. 6Fill the rest works toward the merged five; the button says so. The weapon's search is untouched.
  7. 7Costs on top of round 2: a drawer with selection state and keyboard handling; a Targets field on the build (like Conditions overrides) read by the sheet, the scorer and a fill request. Damage numbers are illustrative.
