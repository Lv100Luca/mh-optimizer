Comparing builds: what's hard today, and four ideas

Two jobs: weapon A vs weapon B in one profile, and the build you wear vs a new one (from the optimizer or made up by hand). I went through both in the current app (main at b274ddc) with the Luca profile. Below are the problems I found, then four mockups. The mockups use your real builds, styled like the app, and their controls work.

What's hard today

Each issue was seen in the app; the screenshots are from the build of today's main.

1
"Compare" on a result takes you away from it, to the wrong spot.

The button opens My builds at the top. The comparison is the last section, about 4,300 px down, below the equipment editor, conditions, targets and the damage table.

2
What you compare against is implicit.

Results' "Differences against" and the My builds comparison both use whichever My builds tab was last selected, so clicking a tab in My builds changes what Results measures against. In My builds the default reference is optimizer build 1, not In game. Results → Compare on build 2 compared it with "Optimizer build 1 (picked)", not with In game.

3
A weapon comparison uses one fixed build per weapon, and it can mislead.

Each side is the weapon's pick (scored now) or else its last run. Current vs Current new reroll showed +1.4 for Current: that was its pick scored today against the reroll's stale run. Both actually reach the same 1,353. With your In game armor, though, Current is ahead by 125 (1,289 vs 1,164), and that isn't shown anywhere.

4
The weapons' own conditions differ without a word.

Current has "Coalescence active" on; Current new reroll has it off. Each side is scored under its own weapon's conditions, and the comparison doesn't mention the difference.

5
You have to find the changes yourself.

Equipment is two lists of names side by side, with the changed rows tinted. Nothing says "swap 3 pieces: arms, legs, talisman", which skills each swap brings, or which result needs the fewest new pieces.

6
Mixed units.

Optimizer cards from an older run show score (681.59, "−1.4 vs …"), while the strip above them and My builds show damage (1,289 / 1,353).

7
Two at a time, and never across weapons.

You can compare two weapons (Inventory) or two builds of one weapon (My builds). You can't put In game on Current next to run build 3 of the reroll, or line up three candidates. The weapon's own stats (rolled pair, attack, slots) aren't in any comparison.

Four ideas

They can be built separately. 2 and 4 cover "the build you wear vs a new one", 3 covers "weapon A vs weapon B", and 1 is the general tool both could open into. Pink dots in each mockup are numbered design notes; untick "design notes" in the top bar to hide them.

Idea 1 · new tab

A Compare tab for the whole profile

Up to five builds from any weapon side by side, against a baseline you choose. All scored on one basis (own conditions, the baseline's, or every condition on). Shows what it takes to get from the baseline to each build, damage per attack, and skill and equipment grids. A compare tray on every tab collects the builds.

 Idea 2 · Results tab

Results measured against your build

"Compare with" becomes its own setting (default: the ★ pick). A table view gives one line per build: damage, gain over your build, which of the seven slots change, and skills gained and lost. Sort by fewest changes to find the cheapest upgrade. Clicking a row opens its swap list in place.

fixes1256
 Idea 3 · Inventory

Weapon vs weapon

One damage axis per weapon type: your builds, the pick and the best of the last run on one line, so the headroom is visible. Tick two weapons for a head to head with a one-sentence verdict, the weapons' own stats, a choice of which build per side, and a check that flags different conditions or stale runs.

fixes347
 Idea 4 · My builds

Try a change on the build you wear

"Try a change" opens a draft of In game, side by side with it. Each swap shows what it alone is worth and can be undone, broken targets show up right away, and you apply, keep or discard the draft. A list of one-piece upgrades from the last run comes with each piece's gain.

fixes125

If I had to pick an order

  1. Groundwork shared by all four: an explicit baseline per weapon, no longer tied to the selected My builds tab; one "what changes" helper (pieces to swap or re-gem, skills gained and lost); damage as the one unit, with stale runs rescored and flagged.
  2. Idea 2: the cheapest change with the biggest daily effect on "the build I wear vs the optimizer's". It reuses the current compare, shown inline.
  3. Idea 3 for weapon A vs weapon B: the head to head with the condition check fixes the misleading +1.4 case.
  4. Idea 1 once comparisons need more than two builds or cross weapons. It needs builds of other weapons scored in the page; the Inventory already does that for picks.
  5. Idea 4 last. It has the most new state (drafts) and the most extra scoring (one evaluation per possible swap).
