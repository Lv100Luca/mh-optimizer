Idea 4 · Try a change on the build you wear

  1. 1Try a change makes a draft of In game that remembers where it came from. Its baseline is In game, not "optimizer build 1" (today's default reference).
  2. 2The score bar stays visible while you edit: the draft's damage, the gain over In game and how each stat moves.
  3. 3Side by side: In game on the left (read-only), the draft on the right, piece by piece.
  4. 4Each changed row says what that swap alone is worth (damage and skills), and ↺ undoes just that row.
  5. 5Targets the draft breaks show up right away (here: Stun Resistance 3 is lost with the talisman).
  6. 6Apply to In game, Keep as a new build or Discard. Drafts don't pile up as "In game copy 3".
  7. 7One-piece upgrades: for each slot, the best piece from the last run that fits with the rest of In game, with its gain. One click puts it in the draft.

The pieces are real; the draft's damage and the per-swap gains are made up to show the layout.
