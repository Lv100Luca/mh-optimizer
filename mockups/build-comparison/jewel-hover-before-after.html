Compare: jewel hover and slot labels

Hovering a jewel

Before: only the browser's plain tooltip, after about a second (not in the screenshot). After: the jewel with its slot level and the card of each skill it carries at its level, like the skill chips. The card is built on the first hover only, so tables with many jewels stay fast. Jewel pickers keep the plain tooltip: their lists already name the skills.

Equipment rows: what the labels mean

Both labels compare a column with the baseline (★). "another piece" is now "other piece than the baseline", "other decorations" is now "baseline's piece, other decorations"; hovering a label says what the baseline wears in that slot.
