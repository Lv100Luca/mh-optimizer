Idea 3 · Weapon vs weapon in the Inventory

  1. 1One damage axis per weapon type. Each weapon shows your builds (●), its ★ pick and the best build of its last run (◆, dashed when stale). The gap between ● and ◆ is what a rebuild would gain.
  2. 2Tick two weapons for a head to head (three or more open the Compare tab). Today the comparison mixes a pick scored now with a stale run, so +1.4 is only staleness.
  3. 3Compare by: the ceiling (best of the run), the same armor (each weapon's In game), the picks, or a build you choose per side.
  4. 4A verdict in one sentence that says what the numbers mean.
  5. 5The weapons themselves side by side: rolled pair, attack, affinity, element, sharpness, slots. Rows that are the same are dimmed.
  6. 6Fairness check: each weapon keeps its own conditions. Here they really differ: Coalescence is on for Current only. One click scores both under the same.
  7. 7Both runs are stale: rerun both before deciding.
