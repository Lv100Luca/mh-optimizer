Idea 4 · Pick the weapon from the tree, in a modal

  1. 1On the Weapon tab in Tech tree mode the dropdown becomes a card with the chosen weapon and a Choose from the tree button. The search dropdown stays next to it for people who know the name.
  2. 2The button opens a modal with the type's tree. The grid scrolls both ways inside it, lane names and tier headers stay put. Search and a rarity filter dim what does not match.
  3. 3Clicking a weapon selects it and shows its details on the right: stats, gauge, skills, this step's cost and the path from scratch. Nothing is applied yet. The lit path and the lineage give context.
  4. 4The footer confirms explicitly: Use Rathalos Firesword. Cancel or Escape closes without a change. The weapon in the footer is the selected one, so the button always says what will happen.
  5. 5Variant B puts the details in a sheet under the tree instead of beside it, so the grid gets the full width (nine tiers fit at 1440 without scrolling). Same confirm.
  6. 6Phone: the modal is full screen, the compact icon grid on top, details as a sheet, the confirm button full width at the bottom.
