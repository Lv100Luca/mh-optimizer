Idea 3 · The forge's tree, rebuilt

  1. 1The API gives every weapon a row (lane) and column (tier) in the forge screen. Laid out as a grid, with a line from each weapon to the one it upgrades from, it is the game's tree: 28 lanes, 10 tiers for Great Sword.
  2. 2Lane labels on the left name the tree. Columns are the tiers; the label is the rarity most weapons in that column have. Both stay put when you scroll.
  3. 3Hover shows the stats, click selects: the path from the forgeable root lights up in gold, everything else dims, and the bar above sums the path's zenny. A second click opens the weapon page.
  4. 4A green edge marks weapons you can forge directly (no previous weapon needed).
  5. 5Variant B is the compact grid: icon only, fits 1080p without scrolling sideways, names in the tooltip. Same data and interactions.
  6. 6On a phone the grid scrolls both ways inside its card; the lane labels stay. Variant B is the better fit there.
