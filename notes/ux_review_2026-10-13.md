# UX review: three builds in Chrome (2026-10-13)

Built three weapons in the browser app (Chrome, profile Luca, localhost:5240) and noted everything that was slow, unclear
or easy to get wrong. Builds first, then findings ranked by impact, then the design rules they point to.

## Status (2026-10-14): all eight findings addressed

1. **Background tab:** the run path yields to the page through `ICpSatBackend.YieldAsync`, which the browser does with
   a MessageChannel instead of a timer. Weapon tuning also yields only after 50 ms of work. The tab title shows the
   run's stage. `tools/ui-shots/background.mjs` times a run in a hidden window. Measured in Chromium after 5.5 min
   hidden (a tuned bow, 10 builds): the old build took 124 s (the tuning step waited 102 s on throttled timers), the
   new one 20.5 s (the tuning step took 0.1 s).
2. **Progress:** a stage line ("Weapon start 2 of 3 (Element focus) · armor search 1 (at most 4) · build 3 of 5 (up to
   10 s)") replaces the raw log line, and the log stays behind its toggle. Log lines read as plain sentences and use the
   cards' damage numbers. Builds whose solve hit the time limit are marked "not proved", with a note per run.
3. **Results:** Cards is the default view and the choice is remembered in the browser. Table rows show sets, armor,
   talisman and skills. "No rolled skill pair" replaces "- + -". The "every condition on" number shows only when it is
   higher, with an explanation. Over-cap points show as "+1 over the max". Bows show their coating instead of
   sharpness.
4. **Weapon page:** a "Let the optimizer choose" switch sits on each part's row, and an optimized part is greyed out
   and can't be edited. Reinforcements are count steppers per type and tier. A saved weapon's type is a chip with
   "Change type". The resolved weapon card stays in view while you scroll. The Element Infusion line has a game-side
   explanation.
5. **Targets:** level buttons on every row ask for the level when you add a skill. Limits from the preset say "from
   the Great Sword preset". "+ Burst ≤ 1" shows only for the Great Sword. Name matches come first; description-only
   matches are listed below them.
6. **Where things live:** new "Attack & target" tab, and "Inventory" is now "Weapons". Options says whose settings
   they are, and storage moved to the Weapons page. The Threads hint and the run log explain the 4 threads per solve.
   "New weapon" always opens the Weapon tab.
7. **Header:** the profile menu sits left of the profile picker, behind a divider. Save as and Delete moved into a
   weapon ⋮ menu. "Save…" saves a new weapon and suggests a name like "Dragon Great Sword". The picker lists weapons
   by type with icons; the unexplained ✓ is gone.
8. **Warnings:** a weapon without a rolled pair no longer warns. The Weapon page explains it in a neutral line.

## Findings

### 1. Runs crawl when the tab is in the background (biggest issue)
- The Fire LS run took **1,860 s**. With the tab visible, the same kind of step took 0.1 s on the bow run. In the
  background it took about 250 s.
- Evidence: log lines land on exact 60.0 s steps (420.3 → 480.3 s, 1140.3 → 1200.3 s) and the elapsed counter only
  moves once a minute. That is Chrome's intensive timer throttling for hidden tabs.
- Cause, very likely: `Optimizer.TuneAsync` does `await Task.Yield()` between builds (Optimizer.cs:277), and in the
  browser that resumes through a timer. Each yield waits up to a minute; 5 builds per round make about 4–5 minutes.
- People will switch tabs during a multi-minute run. Options: run the tuning inside the solver worker, or yield without
  a timer. At least warn "keep this tab in front", and show progress in the tab title.

### 2. Long runs give no sense of progress
- Weapon tuning with Focus + Reinforcements runs up to 3 starts × several rounds. The page only shows "Searching…",
  elapsed seconds and the latest log line. There is no "focus 2 of 3, round 2", no estimate, and nothing for minutes.
- Fire LS solve 1 used its full 120 s without proving the result. The UI doesn't say that this costs quality.
- The log is written in engine terms ("score model: 14 units (…joint)", "tightened: 10 of 18 factors capped"). Its
  scores are on another scale than the cards: the log says 717.57, 645.88 and 580.63; the cards say 337, 1,226 and 1,150.
- For an empty pair it prints "Searching builds for - + -".

### 3. Results: the default view hides what matters
- Table is the default view and it shows only "Build 1…5", damage, ⇄ and Pick: no armor, no sets, no skills. You
  can't choose a build from it. Cards has everything, but the switch isn't remembered: the next result opens in Table again.
- The skill pair header shows "#1 [icon] - + [icon] -" when there is no pair (also "- / -" in Inventory). Write
  "no rolled pair".
- "damage per attack ▸ · every condition on: 1,200" puts a second number next to the score without explaining it.
- Over-cap levels are hidden: Fire LS has 2 × Critical Jewel III (6 points) and shows Critical Boost 5. Show the wasted
  point, or say why the solver accepted it, so it doesn't look like a bug.
- The bow's weapon line says "229 attack, 0% affinity, 420 Dragon, **- sharpness**". Bows have no sharpness; show the
  coating instead.
- Good: the "Focus on this armor" and "Reinforcements on this armor" tables (−1.1%, −4.9%, "All 46") are very clear.

### 4. The Weapon page order fights the task
- The order is type tiles, then "Optimize the weapon", then Stats, then Rolled pair. You read about optimizing parts
  before you have described the weapon. Put each "let the optimizer choose" switch on the row it controls (Focus,
  Element, Infusion, Reinforcements, Pair).
- When a part is optimized, its controls stay live: Reinforcements get the "optimized" chip, but the five dropdowns
  still say "— empty —" and can be edited. Disable them or replace them with "chosen per build".
- The type tiles (14 tiles, two disabled) stay at full size for a saved weapon. After the first choice, collapse them
  to a chip.
- The "Resolved weapon" card isn't sticky. While you edit reinforcements at the bottom, the card you want to watch has
  scrolled away.
- Five reinforcement dropdowns are slow: the popup font is tiny, it covers the neighbouring dropdowns, and you scroll
  to reach Sharpness EX. Count steppers per type and tier (Attack EX [−2+]) would match how people say it: "two Attack
  EX, two Attack III, Sharpness EX".
- The Element Infusion switch is separate from the element, and it isn't clear when it applies in the game ("all three
  parts share the element"). I left it off on the Dragon GS because you didn't mention it; check whether your GS has it.

### 5. Targets: defaults that surprise
- Clicking a skill adds it at its maximum: Constitution became "at least 5" and Arkveld's Hunger became "II · 4 pc".
  For comfort skills like Constitution, the user usually means a lower level. Ask for the level on add, or add at 1
  and let the user raise it.
- The new Great Sword started with "Burst at most 1". It comes from the Great Sword preset (presets hold skill limits),
  but the Targets page doesn't say where it came from. Label it "from the Great Sword preset".
- The quick button "+ Burst ≤ 0 (slow weapons)" also shows on the Bow.
- Minor: the loose search for "speed eat" also lists Quick Sheathe (because its text says "Speeds up").

### 6. Where things live
- "Attack and target" is at the bottom of Conditions, under 30 toggles. That setting decides what "damage" means, so
  it should be its own tab or sit on the Weapon page.
- Options mixes scopes: per-weapon search settings, browser storage for the whole app, allowed armor and excluded
  sets. It isn't clear which ones belong to this weapon.
- Threads reads "All (16)" but the log says "with 4 CP-SAT workers".
- "Inventory" is the list of weapons. "Weapons" would say what it is.
- The Inventory "+ New weapon" asks for the type first (dropdown "Great Sword"); the header picker's "New weapon"
  doesn't. Picking "New weapon" while on Results opens an empty Results page instead of Weapon.

### 7. Header and destructive actions
- The profile ⋮ menu (with "Delete Luca…") sits right next to the weapon picker. I opened it by accident while aiming
  for the picker.
- "Delete" (weapon) sits right next to "Run optimizer".
- On a new weapon, "Save" is disabled while the "unsaved changes" chip shows: you need "Save as…". Make Save act as
  Save as on a new weapon.
- The Save as name defaults to "my-weapon". Prefill something like "Dragon Great Sword".
- The weapon picker lists names with ✓ only, with no type icon. You can't tell which weapons are Great Swords.

### 8. Warnings everyone gets
- Every weapon without a rolled pair (the default for a new weapon) shows a permanent orange status bar and a
  "1 warn" badge: "Skill pair mode is 'fixed' but the weapon has neither set_bonus nor group_skill". It uses internal
  ids and fires in the normal case, so people learn to ignore it. If no pair is a valid state, show a neutral
  "no rolled pair" note.

## Design rules these point to

1. **Show what matters first.** The default results view must show armor, sets and skills. Logs and engine numbers
   belong behind "details".
2. **Put the control next to what it controls.** Optimize switches go on their rows, and the attack goes with the
   weapon.
3. **Say where a value came from.** Preset limits, inherited conditions and over-cap levels need a visible source.
4. **Defaults follow what users mean.** Add targets at a sensible level, name new things after what they are, and
   remember view choices.
5. **Warn only when something is wrong.** Normal states never get orange bars, and messages never use code ids.
6. **Long work explains itself.** Show the stages, a rough estimate, and that the work slows down in a background tab.
7. **Keep destructive actions away from primary ones.** Delete profile and Delete weapon don't sit next to the
   things you click most.
8. **One scale everywhere.** Log, cards and inventory use the same damage number.
